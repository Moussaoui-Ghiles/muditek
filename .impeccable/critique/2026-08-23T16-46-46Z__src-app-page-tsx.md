# Pre-fix visual and conversion critique

Assessment A found strong pricing transparency, held-meeting billing rules, and buyer calculators, but the buyer did not book.

## P1 backlog

- The qualification CTA lands on explanatory copy instead of the final fit action.
- The public skill pages lead with raw repository language and duplicate their H1 headings.
- The outbound diagnostic contains a stale scarcity claim and has no section navigation.
- An unsubscribed newsletter address can be reactivated through the public subscribe endpoint because the stored status is `unsub`, while the consent policy checks `unsubscribed`.
- Newsletter preference bearer tokens reach global analytics and third-party providers through the root layout.
- The Outbound Brief Builder widens the mobile page after it generates a result.

## P2 backlog

- Buyer-facing pages use internal phrases such as front-end offer, back-end capability, and account layer.
- The library headline clips at 390 pixels.
- The mobile menu lets keyboard focus escape behind the open overlay.
- Newsletter fields rely on placeholder text instead of a persistent label.
- Public tool inputs need stable names, IDs, state resets, and stronger helper text.
- The newsletter makes unsupported absolute claims and repeats its signup form.
- Reduced motion does not disable smooth scrolling.

## Strengths to preserve

- Public pricing and source dates.
- Written qualification and no-show rules.
- Browser-only tool privacy.
- Clear public versus account download boundaries.
- Strong keyboard focus indicators and skip link.
