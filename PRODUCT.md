# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
B2B founders, COOs and revenue leads who already use AI every day and are tired of babysitting it. M&A advisors and buyers who need owner meetings. Operators who read the newsletter and use the free library (skills, resources, browser tools) through the portal.

## Product Purpose
muditek.com sells two offers: AI transformation (audit, systems built for you, coaching) and outbound (built and run for you, coaching, M&A origination). Outbound pricing lives on meetingsheld.com. The site's conversions, in order: newsletter subscription, portal account, booked call.

## Brand Personality
Plain, operated, owned. Written by the person who runs the systems. No hype, no invented numbers, no testimonials, no case studies until they exist.

## Anti-references
Generic AI agency slop: gradient text, glass cards, blurred glow blobs, tracked all-caps eyebrows above every section, an italic accent word per headline, identical card grids, stock or AI-generated imagery, "streamline / empower / transform" copy, emerald and sky accents mixed with amber.

## Design Principles
- The hero is the design system: navy, Inter Black, amber as light. Everything below the hero must look like it belongs under it.
- Show the artifact, not the illustration: file trees, reply threads, before/after rows, real library items.
- One accent. Amber is used as light and on the primary button only.
- Every page converts: newsletter capture and a portal path on every marketing page.
- Nothing invented. Words only where numbers are not real yet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast on body text, visible focus, reduced motion respected, no content gated behind scroll animation.
