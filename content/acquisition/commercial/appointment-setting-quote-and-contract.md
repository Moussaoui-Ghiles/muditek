## Start with the invoice trigger

The quote should name the event that creates a charge. "Lead," "appointment," and "qualified meeting" are too broad without an acceptance rule.

Write the rule into the contract or an attached statement of work.

## Contract review checklist

### Price

- Setup charge and what it buys
- Monthly charge and what it buys
- Delivery charge and its billing unit
- Taxes, currency, payment timing, and late-payment terms
- Required software, data, or media bought outside the quote

### Meeting acceptance

- Approved company types, sizes, locations, and exclusions
- Approved attendee roles or authority
- Required problem or service relevance
- Minimum attendance time
- Treatment of no-shows and cancellations
- Treatment of duplicates, current customers, and open opportunities
- Evidence required for acceptance or rejection
- Dispute deadline and credit or replacement rule

### Operation

- Included channels and sending volume
- Ownership of domains, inboxes, lists, copy, and reply history
- Data sources and verification method
- Suppression and opt-out handling
- Reporting frequency and fields
- Buyer approval points and stop rules

### Exit

- Initial term and renewal
- Cancellation notice
- Work and fees after notice
- Export and deletion of buyer data
- Transfer of domains, inboxes, files, and credentials

## Recalculate the economics

Use the complete first-month cost:

`setup + monthly charges + mandatory extras + qualified held meetings × delivery fee`

Then calculate cost per qualified held meeting, expected customer acquisition cost, break-even close rate, and expected gross profit with the buyer's own show, qualification, close, revenue, and margin inputs.

The [appointment-setting quote calculator](/tools/appointment-setting-quote-calculator) performs those calculations without industry defaults.

## Record missing terms as missing

Do not infer a favorable rule from silence. If no-show treatment, qualification, or cancellation is absent, return the quote with a question. A missing term is not proof of a bad provider. It is an unresolved commercial risk.
