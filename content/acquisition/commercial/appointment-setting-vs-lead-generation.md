## The difference is the stopping point

Lead generation produces a defined prospect record or response. Appointment setting continues through scheduling and, in a held-meeting model, attendance and qualification.

The labels are loose. The contract matters more than the service name.

## Compare the delivery events

| Service | Possible billing event | Buyer still needs to do |
|---|---|---|
| Account research | Approved company record | Find and reach a relevant person |
| Contact generation | Approved contact record | Run outreach and handle replies |
| Reply generation | Relevant response | Qualify, schedule, and recover no-shows |
| Appointment setting | Booked or held meeting | Run discovery, propose, and close |
| Qualified opportunity generation | Agreed opportunity event | Propose, negotiate, and close |

Ask the provider to name the exact row it sells. Then define the acceptance rule for that row.

## Choose lead generation when

Choose lead generation when the company already has functioning outreach, reply handling, and sales capacity but lacks accurate account or contact data. It can also fit when the buyer wants direct control of the message and sequence.

The useful quality measures depend on the purchased unit. For contact data, measure account fit, role fit, verification coverage, duplication, and exclusions. Do not judge it by meetings if meetings are outside the scope.

## Choose appointment setting when

Choose appointment setting when the company wants the provider to operate research, outreach, follow-up, reply screening, and booking. The buyer must still have an offer that works in a sales conversation and enough capacity to take calls quickly.

If billing is tied to a qualified held meeting, define company fit, attendee role, relevant problem, attendance time, no-show treatment, duplicates, and disputes before launch.

## Prevent a false price comparison

A price per contact and a price per held meeting buy different work and carry different risks. Convert both options into total cost for the same target outcome.

Use your own contact-to-reply, reply-to-booking, show, qualification, and close rates. If those rates are missing, mark the result as unknown. Do not replace them with an industry benchmark.
