## What the service should replace

A B2B appointment-setting service should own the work between an approved market and a qualified meeting on the calendar. That includes account research, contact data, outreach, follow-up, reply handling, screening, booking, and reporting.

The buyer still owns the offer, sales call, proposal, and close. If nobody can take those calls, more meetings create a new bottleneck.

## Write the acceptance rule first

Do not buy an undefined "appointment." Write one billable rule before outreach starts.

- The company matches the approved industry, size, geography, and exclusions.
- The attendee holds an approved role or has the agreed decision authority.
- The prospect confirms that the service category is relevant.
- The attendee joins and remains for the agreed minimum time.
- Existing customers, open opportunities, vendors, and recent duplicates do not count.
- No-shows do not count.

The provider and buyer should use the same rule for screening, reporting, billing, and disputes.

## Separate operating cost from delivery cost

Ask where each euro goes.

The operating cost pays for the campaign infrastructure. This can include domains, inboxes, data, enrichment, verification, sending software, and reporting. A delivery fee pays for the agreed outcome after it happens.

Use this calculation for every quote:

`first-month cost = setup cost + monthly operating cost + qualified held meetings × fee per meeting`

Then calculate:

`cost per qualified held meeting = first-month cost ÷ qualified held meetings`

Use the same meeting count and the same qualification rule for every provider. Otherwise the comparison is meaningless.

## Questions to ask before signing

1. What exact event creates an invoice?
2. Does a booked meeting count, or must the buyer attend?
3. What happens to no-shows, cancellations, duplicates, and rejected meetings?
4. Who owns the domains, inboxes, data, copy, and reply history?
5. Which channels are included?
6. What does the monthly fee pay for?
7. What is the contract term and cancellation rule?
8. Which report proves that a meeting cleared the agreed standard?
