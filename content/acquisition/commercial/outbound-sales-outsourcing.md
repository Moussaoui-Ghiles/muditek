## Decide what leaves the company

"Outsourced outbound" can mean research only, a managed email campaign, appointment setting, or a complete SDR function. A quote is impossible to judge until the work boundary is written.

Use this ownership map before asking for proposals.

| Job | Buyer | Provider | Shared |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offer and price | Owns | Advises | Approves changes |
| Target market and exclusions | Approves | Researches | Reviews evidence |
| Domains and inboxes | Approves ownership | Configures | Reviews health |
| Data and enrichment | Sets requirements | Sources and checks | Audits samples |
| Copy | Approves | Drafts and tests | Reviews claims |
| Sending and follow-up | Monitors | Operates | Agrees stop rules |
| Reply handling | Supplies escalation rules | Screens and routes | Audits decisions |
| Sales calls and proposals | Owns | Does not own by default | Shares context |
| Revenue attribution | Owns | Supplies source data | Reconciles |

## Keep the commercial event precise

Leads, replies, booked meetings, held meetings, qualified held meetings, opportunities, and customers are different events. The contract should name one billing event and define the evidence required for it.

If the provider bills for meetings, specify company fit, attendee role, relevant problem, exclusions, attendance time, cancellation treatment, and dispute window.

## Keep access and ownership reversible

Write down who owns every domain, inbox, list, suppression file, message, reply history, and report. The buyer should be able to stop the service without losing the records needed to continue sales work or protect contacted accounts.

Do not give a provider unrestricted access to the CRM. Give the minimum access needed or use controlled exports and an exclusion list.

## Review one fixed cohort

Track qualified contacts reached, replies, positive replies, qualified conversations, meetings booked, meetings held, opportunities, proposals, customers, and revenue. Keep the original cohort identifier through the funnel.

If contacts are not reachable, fix data or delivery. If relevant conversations appear but meetings do not, inspect the ask and reply handling. If meetings happen without opportunities, inspect qualification or sales discovery.

Use the [outbound cohort tracker](/templates/outbound-cohort-tracker) to keep the denominator stable.
