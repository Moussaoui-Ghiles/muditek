## Define the deliverable before buying it

"Lead generation" can describe a company record, a contact, a verified email address, a reply, a booked meeting, or a qualified opportunity. These are not interchangeable.

Write the delivery unit in plain language.

## Common delivery units

| Unit | What it proves | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Account | The company matches stated rules | A relevant person can be reached |
| Contact | A person and role were identified | The contact data works |
| Verified contact | A buyer path passed the stated check | The person has interest |
| Positive reply | The person expressed relevant interest | A meeting will occur |
| Booked meeting | Time was accepted | The prospect will attend or qualify |
| Qualified held meeting | The attendee joined and cleared the written rule | The buyer will purchase |

The later the billing unit sits in the funnel, the more delivery risk the provider carries. The buyer still owns the sales call and close unless the contract says otherwise.

## Audit the data

For account and contact delivery, require the source, collection date, fit rule, role rule, verification state, and exclusion result. Do not accept invented emails or unsupported intent labels.

For public buyer signals, require the original source and observation date. A signal can change research priority. It does not prove that the account wants a call.

## Audit duplicates and exclusions

Supply an exclusion list that covers customers, open opportunities, partners, vendors, blocked accounts, and recent contacts. Define the duplicate window before delivery begins.

The contract should say whether a rejected record is replaced, credited, or counted. It should also set the evidence and deadline for a dispute.

## Connect delivery to sales

Give every delivered record a source and cohort identifier. Preserve that identifier when the record enters the CRM, books, attends, receives a proposal, and becomes a customer.

Without that chain, outsourced lead generation becomes a volume report instead of an acquisition channel.
