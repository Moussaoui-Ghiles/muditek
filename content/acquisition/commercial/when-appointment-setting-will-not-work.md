## More meetings cannot repair every sales problem

Appointment setting adds conversations at the top of a sales process. It does not repair a product that buyers do not want, an offer nobody understands, weak discovery, slow proposals, or a team that cannot take calls.

Run these gates before funding outreach.

## Market gate

Count eligible accounts after geography, industry, size, exclusions, customers, open opportunities, and recent contacts. Then check whether each account has a reachable path to an approved buyer role.

If the market supports only a few weeks of activation, use named-account sales or partnerships instead of a continuous volume program.

## Economics gate

Estimate gross profit from one customer, not revenue alone.

`gross profit per customer = customer revenue × gross margin`

Then calculate the close rate needed to recover acquisition cost.

`break-even close rate = cost per qualified held meeting ÷ gross profit per customer`

If the required close rate is not credible for the current sales process, change the economics or stop.

## Offer gate

A stranger should understand the buyer, problem, outcome, mechanism, proof available, commercial risk, and first commitment. Missing proof should stay missing. Do not replace it with a guarantee.

If replies repeatedly ask what the company does, the offer is not ready for more volume.

## Delivery gate

The buyer needs someone who can accept meetings, run discovery, send proposals, follow up, and record outcomes. Define response times and ownership before launch.

If calls are held but no qualified opportunities appear, inspect the meeting rule and sales process. SEO or more outreach will not fix that stage.

## Infrastructure gate

Domains, inboxes, authentication, data, verification, suppression, and reporting must work before scale. A server-delivered event does not prove inbox placement, but missing or broken records are still defects.

Use the [outbound failure diagnostic](/playbooks/outbound-failure-diagnostic) to find the first unsupported stage. Stop there. Do not change five parts of the system at once.
