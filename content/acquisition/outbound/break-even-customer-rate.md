## Definition

**Break-even customers = attributable cohort acquisition cost ÷ first-year gross profit per customer**

**Required customer rate = break-even customers ÷ original cohort entries**

Keep the original cohort unit fixed. Use accounts for named-account outreach and unique prospects for a person-level campaign.

## Required inputs

- Complete attributable acquisition cost
- First-year revenue per acquired customer
- Direct cost to serve those customers
- Original cohort size
- Observation window long enough to see customer outcomes

## Interpret the result

The required customer rate is the share of original cohort entries that must become customers for first-year gross profit to recover the acquisition cost.

Compare it with mature first-party cohorts that used similar market, offer, and operating conditions. If no mature cohort exists, label the comparison unknown.

Break-even does not address cash timing, capacity, risk, or opportunity cost. Review those separately before scaling.
