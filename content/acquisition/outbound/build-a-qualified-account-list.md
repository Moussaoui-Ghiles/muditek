## Start with accounts

Build the company list before collecting people. This prevents a large contact export from being mistaken for a qualified market.

1. Apply the approved account rules.
2. Remove excluded companies.
3. Deduplicate by company domain and legal entity.
4. Record the evidence used to approve each account.
5. Mark every unresolved rule as unknown.

## Add relevant buyers

Find the people who own, fund, evaluate, or block the problem. Keep the role used for approval beside each contact.

One account may need several relevant contacts. Do not count those contacts as separate accounts.

## Verify a usable path

A contact record is not a contact path. Check the business address or the available LinkedIn route according to the campaign standard. Record the verification method and date.

## Quality-control sample

Before launch, review a random sample against the original rules. Reject the batch when researchers interpret a rule differently, sources are stale, exclusions were skipped, or the listed buyer cannot act on the problem.

## Deliverable

The usable list contains approved accounts, relevant buyer roles, verified paths, evidence, dates, exclusions, and unknowns. It does not claim that any account has purchase intent.
