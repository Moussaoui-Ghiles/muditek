## Definition

**Conversation-to-booking rate = unique qualified conversations that produce a booked meeting ÷ unique qualified conversations**

Count each prospect once during the observation window.

## Define a booking

A meeting is booked when the agreed participants accept a calendar event for a specific time. A suggested time, booking-link click, or unanswered invitation is not a booking.

## Preserve context

Record the channel, buyer, offer, reply owner, time to response, unresolved qualification questions, and booking date. These fields help inspect the transition without inventing a cause.

## What to investigate

Review whether the next step was clear, whether the meeting had a stated purpose, whether qualification created avoidable friction, and whether the calendar offered usable times.

Do not optimize booking rate alone. A loose booking rule can increase meetings while lowering attendance or opportunity quality.
