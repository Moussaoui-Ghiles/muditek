## Definition

**Cost per contactable account = attributable research, data, and verification cost ÷ qualified accounts with at least one verified buyer path**

This measures the cost of producing one approved account that can enter the planned campaign.

## Include

- research labor allocated to the batch;
- data and enrichment charges;
- verification charges;
- a consistent share of setup or software cost used for that batch.

Do not include unrelated sales or delivery costs.

## Count the output correctly

The denominator is contactable accounts, not contacts and not email addresses. Count an account once even when it contains several verified buyers.

## Use the result

Compare list sources or research methods using the same qualification and verification rules. Inspect quality before choosing the cheaper source.

The number does not predict replies, meetings, or customers. A database record and a verified route to the intended buyer are different outputs.
