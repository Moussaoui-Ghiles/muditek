## Define one test

A useful outbound cohort shares the same segment, offer, channel, list source, operating conditions, and observation window.

Write these fields before launch:

- account-fit rules and buyer roles;
- offer and commercial terms;
- channel and exact first contact event;
- list source and verification standard;
- planned number of unique accounts or prospects;
- start date and observation window;
- qualification and customer definitions;
- attributable costs;
- stop and review rules.

## Keep the denominator fixed

Cold email begins with unique prospects emailed. LinkedIn begins with unique prospects receiving the predefined first action. Named-account outreach begins with activated accounts.

Do not start the cohort at delivered messages or replies. That removes earlier list and contact-path failures from the result.

## Change one material variable

If the segment, offer, list source, channel, or qualification rule changes, start a new cohort. Do not merge incompatible tests to create a larger sample.

## Decide when to review

Choose an observation window long enough for the normal sales process. Label incomplete cohorts as immature. A weak early count is not a final conversion rate.
