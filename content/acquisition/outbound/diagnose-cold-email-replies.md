## Do not start with a rewrite

Few replies can come from account fit, buyer fit, missing contact paths, technical delivery, timing, the offer, proof, or the message. A reply count alone cannot identify the cause.

## Diagnose in sequence

1. Confirm that the cohort contains qualified accounts.
2. Confirm that the contacts match approved buyer roles.
3. Measure verified buyer-path coverage.
4. Review bounces, authentication, sending configuration, and server-delivery events.
5. Classify replies as automatic, negative, positive, or qualified.
6. Inspect whether the reason for contact connects an observable fact to the problem.
7. Review whether a cold buyer can understand the outcome, method, proof, risk, and commitment.
8. Review the message only after the prior checks pass.

## Keep delivery claims narrow

A server-delivery event does not prove primary-inbox placement, reading, or buyer attention. Authentication is a sending requirement, not evidence that the intended buyer saw the message.

## Make one change

Start a new cohort when you change a material variable. Preserve the failed cohort. Its value is the evidence it provides about a specific configuration.
