## Definition

**Expected gross profit per held meeting = customer rate from held meetings × first-year gross profit per customer**

Use your own mature cohort. Do not use an agency benchmark as a substitute for missing customer data.

## Calculate the inputs

**Customer rate from held meetings = customers ÷ qualified meetings held**

**First-year gross profit per customer = first-year revenue attributable to the customer − direct cost to serve that customer**

Use consistent currency and time period. Exclude revenue that is not attributable to the acquired customer.

## Decision use

Compare expected gross profit per meeting with the complete cost of producing and handling that meeting. Include media or data, research, sending infrastructure, appointment-setting fees, sales labor, and other attributable acquisition costs.

The result is an estimate based on observed inputs. It is not the maximum price you should pay under every cash-flow or risk condition.
