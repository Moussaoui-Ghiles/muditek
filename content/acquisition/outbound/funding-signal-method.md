## Funding is an event, not a need

A funding announcement verifies that financing occurred under the terms reported by the source. It does not prove that the company needs an outbound service.

## Find the downstream change

Research whether the company is now:

- entering a new market;
- adding a relevant sales or operating team;
- launching a product;
- increasing compliance or implementation work;
- changing the buying process connected to your offer.

Use current company evidence for the downstream change. Do not infer it from the funding event alone.

## Record the chain

1. Funding source and announcement date
2. Observable downstream change
3. Connection to the problem you solve
4. Relevant buyer role
5. Recency limit
6. Disqualifying evidence
7. Unknowns

## Contact honestly

Lead with the connected operating change, not congratulations. Ask whether the problem is relevant. Never present funding as proof of intent.
