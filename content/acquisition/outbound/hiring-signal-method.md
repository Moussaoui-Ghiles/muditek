## Hiring can reveal an operating change

A current job posting can show that a company is adding a role, capability, location, or function. It does not prove why the role is open or that the company wants an external provider.

## Qualify the signal

Record:

- exact role and function;
- source URL;
- posting or observation date;
- location and seniority;
- the operating change the description explicitly supports;
- how that change connects to the problem;
- the buyer who owns the decision;
- disqualifiers and unknowns.

One generic posting may be weak evidence. A pattern of roles can be stronger when the connection is explicit and current.

## Avoid invented urgency

Do not claim the company is struggling, understaffed, expanding, or replacing a team unless the source establishes it.

Use hiring to prioritize research and create a truthful question. Measure the resulting cohort against accounts selected without that signal.
