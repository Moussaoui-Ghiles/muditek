## Keep the evidence types separate

Public signals are observable facts such as a job posting, funding announcement, facility record, leadership change, or marketplace activity.

Intent data is a provider's measurement or model of behavior associated with research activity. Its meaning depends on the provider, source coverage, identity resolution, scoring method, and observation window.

Neither proves that a specific company plans to buy your service.

## Evaluate the decision value

For either source, ask:

- What behavior or fact was actually observed?
- At what level was it resolved: person, account, or anonymous visitor?
- When did it occur?
- What commercial problem does it connect to?
- Does it change priority, timing, or the contact reason?
- What would disqualify the account?

## Use the narrow claim

Say that the evidence supports research priority. Do not call it purchase intent unless the underlying behavior directly establishes that claim.

Test signal groups as separate cohorts. Compare downstream qualified conversations and customers. Response rates alone are insufficient.
