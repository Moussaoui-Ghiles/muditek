## Definition

**Market runway = remaining eligible reachable accounts ÷ newly activated unique accounts per period**

An account becomes activated when it receives the first planned outbound action. Contacts and follow-ups do not create new accounts.

## Required inputs

- Deduplicated accounts that still pass the ICP rules
- At least one verified path to a relevant buyer
- Previously activated accounts removed
- Planned number of new accounts activated per week or month

Use the same period in the numerator assumptions and activation rate.

## Example

If 600 eligible accounts remain and the campaign activates 100 new accounts each month, the simple runway is six months.

The result does not predict sales. It estimates how long the defined market can support that activation rate.

## Decision use

A short runway can support lower volume, deeper account research, several relevant contacts per account, or a named-account motion. A longer runway may support more standardization when the offer and economics also fit.

Recalculate when the ICP, data source, exclusion rules, or activation rate changes.
