## Definition

**Meeting attendance rate = qualified meetings held ÷ qualified meetings booked**

Define the attendance window and cancellation rules before measuring. Keep held, cancelled, rescheduled, and no-show outcomes separate.

## What counts as held

Use the signed qualification agreement. At minimum, record whether the required buyer attended and whether the meeting occurred long enough to complete the agreed sales conversation.

A rescheduled meeting remains unresolved until it is held or the observation window closes. Do not silently remove no-shows from the denominator.

## What to investigate

Review the time between booking and meeting, calendar clarity, reminders, buyer understanding of the call, qualification quality, and handoff notes.

Attendance does not prove opportunity quality. Track the next stage separately.
