## Definition

**Qualified opportunity rate = held meetings that pass the agreed opportunity definition ÷ qualified meetings held**

Define the opportunity before the campaign. Do not let the definition change after seeing a weak result.

## Minimum evidence

The opportunity record should state:

- the company still passes the account rules;
- the attendee has an agreed role in the decision;
- the relevant problem was confirmed;
- the next commercial step is explicit;
- disqualifiers and unknowns remain visible.

Interest or a friendly conversation is not automatically an opportunity.

## Use the gap

When held meetings do not become opportunities, inspect qualification, the billable-meeting definition, the handoff, the offer, and the sales conversation.

This rate identifies the transition. It does not assign blame to the appointment setter or closer.
