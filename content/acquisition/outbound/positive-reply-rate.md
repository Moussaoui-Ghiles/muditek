## Definition

**Positive-reply rate = unique prospects with a relevant positive reply ÷ unique prospects with at least one recorded server-delivery event**

Define "positive" before replies are classified. Exclude automatic responses. Count each prospect once.

## Classification rule

A positive reply must express relevant interest connected to the commercial reason for contact. A referral, request for details, or request to speak later may qualify when the rule says so. Courtesy, out-of-office messages, and unrelated answers do not.

Keep the original reply available for review. Do not let an automated label become the only evidence.

## What to investigate

Review company fit, buyer fit, timing, reason for contact, offer, proof, and message. This rate shows the transition that needs review. It cannot prove which input caused the result.

Track qualified-conversation rate separately. Positive interest can still fail the qualification rules.
