## Definition

**Qualified-conversation rate = unique prospects entering a qualified conversation ÷ the predefined channel contact event**

For cold email, the denominator may be unique prospects with a recorded server-delivery event. For LinkedIn, use the exact contact event declared before launch.

## Qualification standard

Write the required account fit, buyer role, problem relevance, and timing conditions before the cohort starts. Record which condition each conversation satisfies.

Unknown is not yes. A title alone does not prove authority. Interest alone does not prove fit.

## Why it is separate

Reply rate counts communication. Qualified-conversation rate counts commercially relevant communication. The gap shows whether replies are reaching the intended market and problem.

## Review

Audit disputed conversations using the original messages. Change the definition only for future cohorts so the current measurement remains comparable.
