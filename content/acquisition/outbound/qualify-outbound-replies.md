## Define the classes first

Classify replies with rules written before the campaign:

- **Automatic:** out-of-office, bounce, or system response.
- **Irrelevant:** does not address the commercial reason for contact.
- **Relevant negative:** the person understood the reason and declined.
- **Relevant positive:** expresses interest connected to the offer.
- **Qualified conversation:** passes the agreed account, buyer, problem, and timing rules.

Do not label a polite response as positive simply because a person replied.

## Review the evidence

For a qualified conversation, record which rule each answer satisfies. Keep unknowns open. Do not infer budget, authority, need, or timing from a job title.

## Route the reply

Reply with the smallest next step that resolves the remaining qualification question. Book a meeting only when the agreed booking conditions are met.

## Measure without hiding leakage

Count unique prospects once in each stage. Keep positive-reply rate and qualified-conversation rate separate. The gap shows whether apparent interest is becoming a commercially relevant conversation.

Review disputed classifications in the weekly cohort review and update the rule only for future cohorts.
