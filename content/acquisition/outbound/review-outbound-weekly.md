## Prepare one cohort table

Bring raw counts, calculated rates, costs, and the underlying evidence for one cohort. Do not combine segments or observation windows.

## Review the chain in order

1. Eligible accounts
2. Verified buyer paths
3. Valid contact attempts
4. Relevant responses
5. Qualified conversations
6. Meetings booked
7. Meetings held
8. Qualified opportunities
9. Customers
10. Gross profit and attributable acquisition cost

Stop at the first transition that needs investigation. Open the underlying records. A rate shows where to look, not why it failed.

## Record one decision

Choose one of four actions:

- continue the cohort unchanged;
- stop because the economics or market do not fit;
- start a new cohort with one material change;
- wait because the observation window is incomplete.

Name the owner, due date, expected evidence, and next review date.

## Do not reward motion

Messages, connection requests, posts, and variants are inputs. Keep them for capacity planning. Judge acquisition through qualified calls held, opportunities, customers, and revenue.
