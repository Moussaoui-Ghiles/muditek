## When to use this motion

Named-account outbound fits a small, valuable, difficult-to-reach market better than indiscriminate volume. The decision depends on market runway, potential customer value, and buyer accessibility.

## Build the account brief

For each approved account, record:

- why the company fits;
- relevant buyer roles and decisions;
- verified contact paths;
- an observable reason for contact;
- source and observation date;
- unknowns and disqualifiers;
- the planned sequence across people and channels.

## Coordinate contacts

Assign one account owner. Plan who is contacted, through which channel, and in what order. Avoid simultaneous messages that make the outreach look uncoordinated.

## Measure accounts, not messages

The cohort entry is an activated account. Track the share with a valid contact attempt, relevant engagement, qualified conversation, held meeting, opportunity, and customer.

## Protect a finite market

Use market runway to set activation volume. When runway is short, increase research depth and contact-path coverage before increasing account volume.
