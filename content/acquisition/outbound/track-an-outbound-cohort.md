## Use one complete chain

Track the cohort from its original entry to the business result:

**Original cohort entry → contact event → relevant response → qualified conversation → meeting booked → meeting held → qualified opportunity → customer**

Each stage counts unique prospects or accounts. Use the unit declared before launch.

## Keep channel events separate

For cold email, record the delivery event reported by the sending system. For LinkedIn, record the exact planned action. Do not combine those events into one "reach" number.

After a qualified conversation begins, both channels can use the same booking, attendance, opportunity, and customer definitions.

## Preserve the source fields

Store segment, offer, list source, channel, start date, and observation window with the cohort. Keep the raw stage counts beside calculated rates.

## Investigate in order

Find the first weak transition in the chain. Inspect the underlying accounts, messages, replies, meetings, or opportunities before assigning a cause.

Do not let a downstream win hide a broken upstream process. Do not let a high activity count stand in for customers.
