## What this measures

Buyer-path coverage asks whether the campaign can make a valid contact attempt to a relevant buyer at each qualified account.

**Verified buyer-path coverage = qualified accounts with at least one verified path to a relevant buyer ÷ qualified accounts researched**

This is an operating definition for comparing your own list sources. It is not an industry benchmark.

## What counts as a path

For email, use a business address tied to an approved buyer role and checked under the campaign's verification standard.

For LinkedIn, identify the intended person and the route available to the campaign, such as an existing connection, connection request, or InMail.

## Audit procedure

1. Freeze the qualified-account list.
2. Deduplicate accounts.
3. Check buyer-role approval.
4. Check each contact path and verification date.
5. Count each account once, even when several paths exist.
6. Report email-only, LinkedIn-only, both, and neither.

## What it does not prove

Coverage does not prove delivery, inbox placement, attention, response, interest, or intent. It identifies how much of the approved account list can enter the planned campaign.
