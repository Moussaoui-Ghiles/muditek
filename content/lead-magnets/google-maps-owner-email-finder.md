Here is the Google Maps owner and email finder, as promised.

[Open it on GitHub]({{ASSET_URL}})

Clone or download the repo, open the folder in Claude Code, and feed it a CSV with two columns: company name and website. It reads each company's site, names the owner only when a page proves it, and keeps that page as evidence. When it cannot prove an owner, it says unknown instead of guessing.

The full guide with the three stages sits here: https://muditek.com/playbooks/google-maps-outbound
