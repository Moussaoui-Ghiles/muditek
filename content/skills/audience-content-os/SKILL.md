# Audience Content OS

Use one framework:

```text
Hook = topic + headline + format
Retain = curiosity carried by lists, steps, stories, or combinations
Reward = satisfy the reason the audience consumed the content
```

## Inputs

Require the complete source, intended audience, channel, desired reader outcome, factual evidence, approved claims, and one relevant next action when an ask is needed.

## Workflow

1. Read the complete source.
2. Separate source rules, source examples, project rules, and editorial judgment.
3. Lock the real topic and the reward the source can deliver.
4. Choose a native channel format.
5. Write a truthful headline that gives a reason to consume.
6. Carry an unresolved question through a list, ordered steps, a real story, or a fitting combination.
7. Answer the question and deliver the promised reward.
8. Check every claim, name, number, and first-person statement against the source.
9. Add one ask only after value, and only when the brief requires it.

Do not invent personal experience. Do not turn a source example into the user's story. Do not create a second content framework.

## Output

Return the finished topic, headline, draft, or correction first. When reasoning is requested, label the basis of each decision accurately.

## Completion gate

Complete when the hook and body promise the same reward, every open question is answered, every factual claim is traceable, and the piece works for a reader who has not seen earlier content.
