# Composition Patterns And Originality Rules

Choose one structure. Do not mix too many.

## Structure Types

### Workflow

Use for input to processing to output, content systems, AI workflows, automation chains.

Draw inputs on the left, a weird machine or the black creature processing in the middle, output on the right. Use orange arrows for the main flow.

### System Slice

Use for sources, filters, databases, rendering engines, agent systems, and one piece of a larger workflow.

Draw only 3-5 core modules. The creature must operate one key module.

### Before And After

Use for messy to clean, manual to automated, scattered to focused, anxious to stable.

Draw left messy, right stable, orange movement between them.

### Role State

Use for user pain, creator state, tool overload, information anxiety, stuck to moving.

Draw 2-4 small states with short labels.

### Concept Metaphor

Use for offer machines, content factories, research wells, black boxes, delivery systems, proof loops.

Draw one strange main object or machine, a few inputs, and one output. Make it memorable.

### Method Layers

Use for method frameworks, capability stacks, delivery layers, scoring layers.

Draw hand-built layers or awkward stacked objects. Avoid a formal pyramid.

### Route Map

Use for idea to launch, buyer journey, content path, delivery path.

Draw a crooked path with a few nodes. The creature should walk, pull, repair, or guard the path.

### Small Comic

Use for failure to success, real process, short critique, before to after.

Draw 2-4 tiny panels. One action per panel.

## Fresh Metaphor Method

1. Convert the abstract idea into a physical action: stuck, leaking, heavy, sorted, pressed, folded, repaired, stamped, routed, scored, tightened.
2. Convert the system into a low-tech object: broken machine, paper box, drawer, pipe, mail slot, crooked scale, well, ladder, weird workstation.
3. Make the black creature perform the action: pull, carry, push, sort, weigh, tighten, stitch, cut, guard, catch, connect, mark, recycle.

Use only 1-2 main objects. Do not clutter the canvas.

## Do Not Copy Old Compositions

Do not open or copy `assets/examples/` by default. They are only for occasional visual calibration: line density, whitespace, color restraint, and creature attitude.

Do not reuse these known compositions unless the user explicitly asks:

- Conveyor belt with two breakpoints.
- Creature pulling a judgment lever inside a content machine.
- Creature as a funnel sorting traffic, trust, or conversion.
- Creature cutting a material fish.
- Creature pulling a delivery path.
- Creature pulling three information sources.
- Three creatures holding a megaphone, bridge, and door.
- Creature stamping a copy toolbox.
- Creature holding a warning sign on a common-mistakes path.

For similar themes, invent a new object and action.
