# QA Checklist

## Must Pass

- 16:9 horizontal.
- Clean pure white background.
- Small black creature is present.
- The creature performs the core action, not decoration.
- No Chinese text anywhere.
- English labels are short, sparse, and readable.
- The image uses a fresh metaphor for the current content.
- Weird, clean, and memorable.
- Main subject stays under roughly 60% of canvas.
- One image explains one core structure.
- Orange is only for main path or arrows.
- Red is only for warnings, weak points, problems, or results.
- Blue is only for supporting notes, evidence, feedback, or system state.

## Failure Signals

Regenerate or edit if any of these appear:

- Chinese characters or Chinese labels.
- Top-left title like "Workflow", "System Architecture", "Common Mistakes", or "Roadmap".
- Creature looks like a mascot, sticker, emoji, or children's cartoon.
- Image looks like a PPT slide, course slide, formal flowchart, or dense business infographic.
- Too many elements, arrows, nodes, or labels.
- Text becomes full explanatory paragraphs.
- Background has paper texture, shadow, gradient, beige tint, or grain.
- Real UI screenshot or tech-dashboard look.
- Labels are unreadable or misspelled.
- Image is stiff and lacks an absurd metaphor.
- Composition is too close to `assets/examples/`.

## Iteration Moves

- Too ordinary: make the black creature perform the core action and use a stranger low-tech object.
- Too complex: delete nodes; keep one action and 3-5 short labels.
- Too cute: emphasize deadpan, blank serious expression, not cute, not mascot.
- Too PPT: remove title, grid, boxes, and excessive arrows; turn it into a hand-drawn scene.
- Too close to examples: keep the core idea but replace the main object and creature action.
- Text problems: edit locally when minor; regenerate with fewer labels when severe.

## Delivery Test

A strong image feels weird first, then becomes clear within one second. If it looks like a tutorial slide instead of a strange product sketch on white paper, it fails.
