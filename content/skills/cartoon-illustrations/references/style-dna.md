# Style DNA

## One Line

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, sparse, weird, deadpan, product-sketch style. Clear structure without becoming an instructional diagram.

## Must Have

- 16:9 horizontal body illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradient, shadow, grain, or vintage paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art: thin, slightly wobbly, not mechanical, not vector-clean, not heavy outlined.
- Lots of whitespace: main subject around 40%-60% of canvas, at least 35% blank space.
- Short English handwritten annotations only: max 5-8 labels, usually 1-4 words each.
- One image explains one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- The structure should be implied by the scene; do not write the structure type on the image.

## Language

- English-only by default.
- No Chinese characters.
- No Chinese labels.
- No bilingual labels unless the user explicitly asks.

## Color

- Black: main line art, creature, objects, frames, main labels.
- Red: key warning, weak point, problem, emotional pain, or result.
- Orange: main path, flow, arrow, movement from A to B.
- Blue: supporting note, system state, feedback, evidence, or second-layer context.

Use color sparingly. Blue is optional.

## Never

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographic.
- No formal flowchart.
- No course slide.
- No cute cartoon poster.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagram.
- No polished flat vector art.
- No tech UI aesthetic.
- No realistic app screenshot.
- No complex background, gradient, shadow, or texture.
- No exhaustive node-by-node explanation.
- No top-left title like "Workflow", "System Architecture", "Common Mistakes", or "Roadmap".

## Aesthetic Direction

Make it strange, sharp, clean, and memorable. It should feel like a smart product operator drew a weird explanation on a whiteboard.
