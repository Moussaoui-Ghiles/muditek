### Exact main post

~~~text
How I run your entire lead generation and outbound motion with Claude Code.

The mistake 99% do is giving Claude access to your GTM tools and expecting it to figure out the rest.

I let AI handle the targeting once. Weak research went in, so it invented the prospect’s pain, treated guesses as buying signals, and contacted leads outside my ICP. The messages looked personalized. They were outbound slop built on fiction.

5 steps for a reliable setup:

1. Encode your GTM strategy before connecting any tools.

Define your target market, ICP, buyer roles, disqualifiers, buying signals, cold/frontend offer, approved proof, messaging rules, and performance benchmarks if you have data.

Add example data of accounts you approved, messages you sent, and results you want repeated.

The Agent can now executes your GTM strategy instead of inventing one while the campaign runs.

2. Define how every GTM job should run and recover.

Every job needs three rules:

• PRIMARY: Which tool runs first?
• FALLBACK: Which tool runs if the first one fails?
• PASS RULE: What must be true before the result is accepted?

For email enrichment, CC can start with the lowest-cost provider, try a wider provider after a miss, and accept only a verified business email.

If every provider fails, the field stays “not found.” CC never invents the email or put a personal one.

3. Make account qualification consistent and explainable.

Keep account data separate from qualification judgment.

Headcount, industry, location, funding, hiring, and technology are facts.

ICP fit, buying intent, urgency, and account value are decisions made through your scoring rules.

CC saves the score and the reason behind it. Review the five highest-scoring accounts first. If they are wrong, fix the rule before paying to enrich or contact hundreds more.

4. Build the campaign and route replies.

CC takes the qualified list and approved copy, builds the campaign in Instantly or Smartlead, then leaves it in draft.

Review the targeting, variables, follow-ups, and sending safeguards once before launch.

After launch, it routes every reply:

• Unsubscribe → suppress
• Out of office → reschedule
• Not now → create a dated CRM task
• Referral → verify the new contact
• Interested, unclear, or sensitive → human review

5. Measure qualified pipeline, not activity.

Join campaign data to CRM outcomes. Track qualified pipeline per dollar by source, segment, buying signal, provider, and campaign.

Use those outcomes to replace weak providers, demote signals that create no pipeline, cut losing segments after enough volume, reuse messages that create qualified conversations, and turn manual reply corrections into new rules.

You do not need to rebuild your outbound. Start with the handoff slowing it down. Prove Claude Code makes it better, then expand.

https://muditek.com/portal/playbooks/claude-code-lead-gen-guide
~~~
