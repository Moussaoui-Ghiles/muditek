# X Content Writer

Use one source and one topic. Preserve every material fact and choose the smallest X format that can deliver the source's real payoff.

## Inputs

Require:

- the complete source or approved brief;
- topic;
- factual inventory;
- reader stake when the source establishes one;
- locked wording and rejected directions;
- any required link or media.

## Format routing

- **Short post:** one complete observation or lesson.
- **Long post:** one argument, system, or ordered method. This is the default for material that needs explanation.
- **Article:** an evergreen multi-section reference.
- **Thread:** genuinely parallel lessons that need separate numbered space.

Do not split one argument into a thread for reach. Do not invent a stake when the source does not provide one.

## Workflow

1. State the topic in one sentence.
2. List every usable fact, number, named entity, step, constraint, and artifact.
3. Identify the reader consequence that the source supports. Keep it unknown when absent.
4. Choose the native format.
5. Write one hook that names the topic and uses a real specific when available.
6. Build the body from the factual inventory in source order.
7. Remove any line that serves an adjacent topic.
8. Trace every claim and first-person statement to the source.
9. Add one ask at most, after value, when requested.

## Output

Return the finished piece in copy-ready form. Do not publish it. Do not fabricate a quote URL, experience, client result, or business number.
