## Method: identify, build, adopt

1. **Identify.** A workflow audit with the people doing the work. Every activity tagged automate, augment, or keep human, ranked by impact. If it finds nothing worth building, the audit is free.
2. **Build.** The three folders that make a business AI-executable, then the automations on top, inside the tools the team already uses.
3. **Adopt.** Team training, a keep-it-running playbook, optional retainer. The client owns everything.
