## How We Work

1. **Audit first, build second.** Every engagement starts with a workflow audit. If it finds nothing worth building, the audit is free.
2. **Operated, not handed off.** Outbound is run by Muditek and paid on meetings held. Systems come with training and an optional retainer.
3. **Client owns everything.** Files, prompts, workflows, domains, inboxes, lists, accounts.
